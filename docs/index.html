About the author

Nilay Shah

I built Portfolio Ledger for myself — I was frustrated with every other tool requiring an account, tracking my data, or locking me into a particular brokerage. I wanted something I could open in a browser tab, fill in real numbers, and close without worrying about where those numbers went.

Building the projection engine also forced me to actually understand the math behind compound interest and step-up SIPs — which turned out to be the most valuable part of the project. I hope it helps you think more clearly about your own money too.

If you find a bug, have a suggestion, or just want to say hello — reach out.
