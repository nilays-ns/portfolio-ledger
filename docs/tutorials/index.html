Tutorials

Six walkthroughs for six different types of investors. Each one builds a realistic plan from scratch — pick the one closest to your situation and follow along with the calculator open.

Beginner

Fresh graduate

Age 22–24. First job, small but regular SIP, long runway ahead. Learn the basics — asset table, contribution plan, and what compound interest actually means over 25 years.

Read walkthrough → Intermediate

Mid-career investor

Age 32–35. Multiple assets (SIP, PF, gold, stocks), a growing income, and room to step up contributions. Learn to model a diverse portfolio with the step-up feature.

Read walkthrough → Advanced

Near retirement

Age 48–52. Large existing corpus, fewer earning years left. Focus on the inflation toggle and the "stopped today" chart — understanding what your money is really worth.

Read walkthrough → Global

Foreign currency investor

Investing in USD or EUR assets — US equities, international ETFs, or NRE deposits. See how the tool works in any currency, and how to think about exchange rate effects.

Read walkthrough → High risk

Active trader

High-conviction bets in individual stocks and crypto alongside a core portfolio. Model aggressive return assumptions and understand the outsized impact of volatility.

Read walkthrough → Conservative

Safe investor

FDs, PPF, and government bonds only. Low-risk, predictable returns. See how a conservative portfolio compounds over time and what inflation does to "safe" money.
