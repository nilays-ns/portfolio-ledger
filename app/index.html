Model: each asset compounds monthly at the return you set. New monthly money splits across assets by the "% of monthly investment" column (these are read relative to each other, so they don't need to add up to exactly 100) and stays there — it isn't rebalanced afterward. Contributions stay flat for the first stretch you set, then step up by the amount you set, on the cycle you set.

This is a planning projection, not a forecast or investment advice. Real returns for equities, gold, silver, crypto or private company shares will be far less smooth than a constant annual rate implies, and nothing here accounts for taxes.
